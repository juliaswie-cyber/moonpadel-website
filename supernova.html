Description

Balanced aggression for players who defend, construct and finish the point.

Supernova blends explosive attacking capability with refined control. Its responsive 15K EU Carbon Fibre construction creates a crisp and energetic response for players who attack with intent, while the Black Soft 28 EVA Foam maintains comfort and composure during defensive exchanges.

Fast through the air and exceptionally balanced in hand, Supernova is designed for players who construct points in defence before finishing it with confidence at the net. The result is a racket that feels explosive when attacking, yet controlled in defence.

Supernova wears MoonPadel's signature chameleon finish - a striking two-tone colourway that shifts and shimmers between purple and green with every turn in the light, paired with a sleek carbon face for a look as electric as the racket's performance.
